Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Chess: Learn and Play, a mobile chess app developed by developedbyjacob.

The app allows users to play chess locally against the computer, play local matches between two players on the same device, practice chess endgames, and learn and practice chess openings. The app does not include online multiplayer, user accounts, chat, cloud sync, or social features.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Chess: Learn and Play does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app stores basic gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as a saved game, settings and preferences, and your progress in the endgame and opening lessons. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to any server.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads.

Because the app’s audience includes children, every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment and limited to content suitable for all ages (rated “G”). As a result, the app only shows non-personalized ads: ads are not based on the user’s interests or past behavior, and the advertising ID is not used for personalized advertising or remarketing.

To serve these ads, AdMob may still process limited technical information, such as the device type, operating system, app version, approximate (coarse) location derived from the IP address, and diagnostic information, for purposes such as delivering and measuring ads, frequency capping, and preventing fraud and abuse.

This data is processed by Google according to its own privacy practices. You can learn more about how Google uses data for advertising here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

For Android apps using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google provides information about data handling and Google Play data disclosure requirements here:
https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Non-Personalized Ads Only

Personalized ads use information such as the advertising ID or app activity to show ads based on a user’s interests. This app does not request personalized ads. The ads shown are non-personalized, which means they are based only on contextual information, such as the app content or general location.

Because no personalized advertising takes place, the app does not show an advertising consent form.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for a general audience that includes children, and it participates in the Google Play Families program. We have configured the app to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and with children’s privacy laws such as the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, chat, or sharing features, and it does not ask for a name, email address, photo, contacts, or precise location.

All users are treated as children for advertising purposes: every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment, ads are non-personalized, and ad content is limited to material suitable for all ages. Google AdMob is a self-certified ads SDK under the Google Play Families Policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions about your child’s use of the app, please contact us using the details below.

Local Gameplay

The app supports local chess gameplay only. This means that matches between two players take place on the same device. The app does not transmit moves, player names, game history, or gameplay data to other players or online servers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses one third-party service, which processes limited technical information to serve non-personalized ads as described above.

Third-party service provider used by the app:

  • Google AdMob

Google’s privacy policy is available here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Since the app does not have its own server or account system, we do not directly share user-provided personal data with third parties.

Google AdMob processes limited technical information to serve non-personalized ads, as described above.

Data Retention

Gameplay settings, preferences, and local progress may remain stored on your device until you delete them, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.

Advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s own policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app safe and to avoid collecting unnecessary data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can control certain privacy options through your device settings, including:

  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID.
  • Clearing the app’s local data, which removes saved games, settings, and lesson progress.
  • Uninstalling the app.
